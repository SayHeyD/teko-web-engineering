What is BMI?

The Body Mass Index (BMI) is a standardized screening metric used to evaluate body weight relative to height. It serves as a practical assessment tool to categorize individuals into weight classifications, including underweight, normal weight, overweight, and obesity.

Calculation Formula

BMI is calculated by dividing body weight in kilograms by the square of height in meters:

BMI = weight (kg) / (height (m))²
